Know the limits of the result

  • Restricted C subset; no general pointer, alias, floating-point or invariant support.
  • Small adapted kernels, one clock/configuration and one library corner.
  • Pre-layout cell area and ideal-wire timing; external host/memory excluded.
  • RTL simulation is finite; C-to-C proof does not prove the HLS compiler.
  • Main cohort is curated; local Qwen sampling is a separate case study.

Next steps: symbolic memory, loop invariants, larger workloads and controlled LLM repair experiments.

  • Agreement at one proposed input never proves universal equivalence.
