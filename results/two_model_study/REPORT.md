The area-latency product is cell area × execution latency at a common 10 ns clock. Negative changes are improvements. Useful gains require a changed, proved-equivalent candidate, successful RTL simulation/mapping/timing, at least 0.1% lower area-latency product, and meeting the clock constraint. Unchanged programs count as equivalent but not useful optimizations. Unsupported/inconclusive outcomes are not confirmed semantic errors.

Hardware uses Bambu, Verilator, Yosys and OpenSTA with Nangate45 typical. These are mapped pre-layout ASIC estimates, not routed timing or FPGA LUT counts. Critical-path delay and area are retained in summary.json. Six compute adaptations define new reduced unsigned-integer domains; six leaves preserve their original types and full input domains. They are not twelve full unmodified benchmark applications.

Prompts, local model version/weights, first raw responses, unmodified candidates, formal obligations, native checks, hardware sources/testbenches/logs and per-case dossiers are retained. The main 50-pair study and earlier pilot remain separate. Geometric means in summary.json apply only to the explicitly counted measured candidates; unresolved cases have no fabricated hardware values.

## What the outputs show

Qwen preserves all twelve examples, but nine are unchanged after canonical parsing. Its other edits include compound-assignment spelling, integer-mask/type spelling, and replacing a GEMM local accumulator with output-array accumulation. A changed AST is not necessarily a useful optimization.

GPT-6.1-Sol makes more extensive loop/unrolling and algebraic edits. ATAX and BiCG remain inconclusive at the frozen60-second per-obligation budget: const-qualified locals are outside the specialized symbolic engine, and the remaining ESBMC problems time out. They are not called incorrect and are not synthesized. The checker, source candidates and verification budget were not changed to turn these cases into wins.

The SYRK candidate factors alpha outside the dot product and writes each lower-triangle result once. GEMM, stencil and MVT show why lower latency need not give a lower area-latency cost: exposing more arithmetic can increase mapped area. GSM absolute value trades more area for a shorter critical path.
